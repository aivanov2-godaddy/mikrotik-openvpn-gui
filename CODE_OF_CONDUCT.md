# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be welcoming, respectful, and useful
for operators, contributors, and maintainers of every background and experience
level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Focus feedback on code, documentation, and observed behavior.
- Protect other people's security and privacy: do not share credentials,
  personal data, private keys, production network details, or exploit material.
- Assume good intent and ask for clarification before escalating disagreement.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, deliberate
disclosure of sensitive data, and disruption of project discussions are not
acceptable.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, contact a maintainer through a private GitHub channel. For
security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead. Reports
will be handled discreetly and in good faith.

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation
when necessary to protect people and the project.
